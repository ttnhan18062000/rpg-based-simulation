# The free-meal removal: evidence and the divergence text as drafted (parked)

By owner ruling (2026-10-08) the batch lands with free meals ON; the removal waits on branch `d27-free-meal-removal` until a second way to
eat works in all three measured worlds (earn, or wild land in `crowded_frontier` and `urban_political`). The text below is the divergence entry
as drafted when the removal was part of the batch; its 5000-tick table is the evidence for why it waits (starvation wave in ticks 1000 to 2000:
`urban_political` 0.0 alive at t=2500, `crowded_frontier` 1.0 at t=5000, with the removal on and free meals at 0).

### 2.89 Hunger With No Open Way Pulls Toward the Step That Opens One, a Meal Is Paid or Not Served, and Wild Food Belongs to the Land (TCK-20261007-A-NEED-WITH-NO-OPEN-WAY-PULLS-TOWARD-THE-STEP-THAT-OPENS-ONE-SURV-07-AMENDMENT, TCK-20261007-EAT-BESIDE-THE-INN-FEEDS-A-SUBJECT-THAT-CANNOT-PAY-FREE-MEAL)

- **Landing**: unpushed; the landing order is the owner's (rulings relayed 2026-10-08, Decision 31: it lands once broke people have at least one working way to eat besides the inn). This entry is the Decision 27 + free-meal part of the batch; 2.90 and 2.91 are the other two parts.
- **Legacy Behavior**: `CoreActions.execute_survival("EAT")` cut hunger by 40 with no building and no gold, and the inn's 5-gold charge clamped at 0, so a broke subject ate for free (SURV-06: "no engine charity"). In the pinned base arm, 11.2, 30.6 and 32.8 EAT executions per run were free meals (`crowded_frontier`, `frontier_living_world`, `urban_political`). A hungry subject with no way to pay had no goal that pointed at anything.
- **New Behavior** (ticket TCK-20261007-EAT-BESIDE-THE-INN-FEEDS-A-SUBJECT-THAT-CANNOT-PAY-FREE-MEAL): a purchased town service is paid in full or not delivered (`conservation._service_price`, a negative `gold_delta` counts as a price; a TAX keeps its clamp); an EAT with no inn consumes a carried food item together with the meal (`CARRIED_FOOD` transfer, `core_actions.carried_meal`); `EatScorer` offers the inn only to a subject that can pay (`EAT_PRICE_GOLD`); a hungry subject holding food eats it in place when no present threat holds (`tactical_rest.in_place_survival_update`). Free meals are 0 in every measured run.
- **New Behavior** (ticket TCK-20261007-A-NEED-WITH-NO-OPEN-WAY-PULLS-TOWARD-THE-STEP-THAT-OPENS-ONE-SURV-07-AMENDMENT, Decision 27): a hunger goal with no affordable way carries a typed `NeedAccess` and pulls toward the first open opening step (`ai/goals/opening_steps.py`: forage, then earn-then-buy and ask, both stubs that report why they are closed) with the SURV-07 escalation for the hunger on arrival; with no open step it keeps the raw hunger and `no_open_step=True`. `need_paths` counts food nodes.
- **New Behavior** (Decision 29, wild food): a `berry_thicket` kind (item `wild_berries`, 30 hunger) is declared per wild module: `near_forest` and `sacred_grove` 10 charges (the `wood_node` default, the kind the near-forest ecology already lists), the swamp border 8 (the `herb_patch` default), regen 1 charge per ecology cycle (the schema default; the density modifier is inert at that rate, `ecology.py` rounds to at least 1). The frontier-village biome gets none: the three modules with that biome (`frontier_village_core`, `settled_quarter`, `trading_company_hub`) are all settlement land. The kind carries `placement: owned_by_declared_region` (a content field, `ResourceDefinition.placement`): the compiler redraws from the same seeded stream, at most 64 draws, until the tile is owned by the declared region by region precedence (`first_region_at`); if none is found no node is placed and a warning is appended to the compile warnings. Proof that a kind without the field is untouched: for all 24 resolved worlds at seeds 42 and 43 the compiled state with the rule on and with it off is identical once the food node is removed (`test_the_placement_rule_changes_only_the_food_node`, three worlds; the 24-world check was run by hand). Before the rule all five `frontier_living_world` thickets (seeds 42 to 46) stood on tiles `trading_hometown` owns; after it they stand at (86,16), (89,23), (85,13), (89,23), (86,16), all owned by `near_forest`.
- **Worlds with no wild land**: `crowded_frontier` and `urban_political` compose no wild module, so they have no forage (reported, not added, by ruling). The four inn-less worlds (`dungeon_crawl`, `mechanic_scenario_combat_judgement_withdrawal`, `quest_dense_frontier`, `wilderness_survival`) gain no inn path from this.

- **Reading**: closing the free meal and giving broke people only the forage step raises starvation on all three worlds (the first two batch rows); the missing half is a way to earn and then buy (the shop and blacksmith paths have never run in a compiled world; all 14 `town_council` workers start with 0 gold and earn 5 to 40 gold per 1500-tick run), not more food. Food is never sized to demand (Decision 29).
- **Found, not fixed** (P3): `FatigueScorer` targets the inn's 10-gold rest regardless of gold (1 to 7 rejected `SERVICE_FEE`/`TAX` transfers per run, no meal attempts); a staged `ENTITY_MOVE` onto a node tile keeps harvesting and keeps the brain from running (no live task kind is known to do this); the price law conflict between `ShopService.buy_item` (`DynamicPriceService`) and `MarketSystem.calculate_price` is a parity gap for the earn-then-buy build.
- **Re-baseline, disclosed**: every subject's trajectory that hungers, walks to the inn or gathers changes; SimQ pillars reading survival, movement, combat or harvest, the corpus baselines and `grade_anchors.json` can move and are not re-measured here. The 200t/500t/1000t grade-stability tests in `tests/unit/worldassembly/test_corpus_diversity.py` fail in about the same number on main and on this branch under load (12 and 11 failures plus one error each, 33 and 24 minutes); they are timing-dependent, not attributed to this change.
- **Rationale**: **Intentional Gameplay Change** (Decisions 27 and 29, SURV-06/SURV-07) and **Bug Fix** (the free meal and the unpaid service).
