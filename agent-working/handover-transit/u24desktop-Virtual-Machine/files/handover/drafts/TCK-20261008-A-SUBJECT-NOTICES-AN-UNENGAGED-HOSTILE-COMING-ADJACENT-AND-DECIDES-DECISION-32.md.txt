# TCK-20261008-A-SUBJECT-NOTICES-AN-UNENGAGED-HOSTILE-COMING-ADJACENT-AND-DECIDES-DECISION-32

## Title
A subject notices when a perceived hostile it is not fighting comes adjacent and decides right then: fight, step away, wait, or keep walking and knowingly pay the opportunity attack (owner decision 32, CONFLICT-04 scope).

## Status
OPEN

## Tier
standard

## Type
feature

## Priority
P2

## Request Summary
Owner decision 32 (decided by the user directly, 2026-10-08, via rpg-designer). It is a CONFLICT-04 amendment and memo row 32, on the designer's milestone branch `milestone-d27-earn-then-buy` at commit 6eba4582b.

Evidence is Lane A's three-arm pinned 5x3 (crowded / living / urban), from the free-hit batch:
- Opportunity-attack hits: base 185 / 158 / 87. With the engaged-only movement rule (the one shipped) 180 / 123 / 65. With any adjacent hostile 14 / 37 / 8.
- With any adjacent hostile, entities freeze for 137 to 219 ticks beside a hostile that never attacks them, because the brain never re-decides. A freeze at the movement layer was rejected (the alternative not taken).
- After engaged-only, about 90 percent of the remaining free hits come from walking past an unengaged adjacent hostile with no decision made.

Rule: the adjacency event (the subject walks up, or the hostile approaches) prompts a fresh decision at that time. The decision may be to keep walking and pay the cost, but it has to be taken.

## Scope
1. Design (proposed to rpg-planner before building): how an "unengaged perceived hostile became adjacent" event makes the brain decide on that tick, or the next one, for an entity that is otherwise held (a held move or an idle walk) or between brain cadences. Candidates:
   - an event-driven brain wake in the scheduler's readiness or brain-eligibility selection;
   - an interrupt that releases the held task back to the brain (as #430 and #429 do for other cases). See the parked WIP `rpg-opportunity-attack` 7f2455353 (`src/engine/held_move_threat.py`). Its first five-seed result showed no reduction, because the released entity's idle task walked on the strategic target again; the engaged-only movement rule now changes that.

   Constraint: this TOUCHES SCHEDULING. Scheduler, kernel and policy edits need rpg-planner's explicit approval, and perf #415 must be told. Prefer the release-to-brain shape (no scheduler change) if it meets the rule.
2. The decision itself: the brain's existing terms (present threat AGENCY-07, CONFLICT-04's hold, goal priority) choose between fight, step away, wait and keep walking. No new tuning dial.
3. Kernel scenario with a control arm: a walker passing an idle hostile decides on the adjacency tick (logged decision) and acts on it; the control (no hostile) walks on undisturbed.

## Out of Scope
- A freeze at the movement layer (rejected by decision 32).
- Engaged adjacency. It is already handled by CONFLICT-04 (#439) and the engaged-only movement rule in Lane A's free-hit batch.
- Hazard-aware or threat-aware pathing (feature work, frozen by row 7).

## Acceptance Criteria
- Every adjacency event with an unengaged perceived hostile is followed by a brain decision for that subject within a bounded number of ticks (state the bound). A pinned-run invariant counts the violations: 0.
- Pinned 5x3, main vs feature, with mean (SD) of: OA hits by class, deaths by class, alive at t=1000/1100, freeze entity-ticks and the longest freeze, brain decisions per run (cost), and tick wall time on one seed. Two-run digests.
- The divergence entry is an Intentional Gameplay Change (decision 32). Add a parity entry, a Bible 02 §7 line, and the tactical contract.
