# TCK-20261009-MINTED-KILL-AND-QUEST-COIN-IS-PAID-BY-A-PAYER-EXCH-02-B

## Title
Kill bounties and quest rewards are paid from a real payer (the posting faction's treasury, the quest giver's purse), and loot is only what the dead body carried; no coin is minted (EXCH-02 (b), owner ruling 2026-10-09).

## Status
OPEN

## Tier
standard

## Type
feature

## Priority
P2

## Request Summary
Today `COMBAT`/`KILL_REWARD` and `QUEST`/`QUEST_REWARD` transfers grant gold with no payer debited (`engine/quests.py` about line 210, `quest_opportunity_rewards.py`, conservation grants). This is the only coin reaching town workers (5 to 35 gold per run). The owner ruled (via rpg-designer; recorded as a decision on the milestone branch) that a payer is needed, as its own ticket AFTER first coin lands. Until then the minting is recorded as a known exception (an intentional_divergences entry, class Bounded) that names this ticket.

## Scope
1. Map every minting transfer kind and its call sites.
2. Design for rpg-planner: which payer each one has (the faction that posted the bounty, the quest giver), what happens when the payer cannot pay (no reward, or a debt? a designer reading), and loot as the corpse inventory only.
3. Build with conservation tests; measure the coin flow per run and the deaths/alive effect (pinned 5x3).

- Also in scope (designer audit, 2026-10-09): the faction-vault building tax MINTS +10 per functional building per tax tick (`town_resolution.py` section 4), and maintenance of -5 goes to no one. Both need a payer or payee. The compiler defaults every treasury to 1000 (`compiler.py:535`). Legacy Faction-enum vault keys may collide (inferred): verify.
- Also (Lane B, 2026-10-09): a REPAIR's gold_cost is charged on top of its gold_delta (a repair costs double its price), and a repair with no blacksmith in reach pays an unnamed payee (the coin vanishes). Decide whether the price is intended, and give every repair a payee.

## Out of Scope
- First coin (Lane B batch 2).

## Acceptance Criteria
- No transfer creates gold (a conservation test over a pinned run: total gold is constant except declared world-creation coin).
- The Bounded divergence entry is retired.
