# TCK-20261009-THE-FACTION-TREASURY-PAYS-WAGES-FOR-TOWN-WORK-DECISION-37

## Title
A town's controlling faction treasury pays a wage for town work (public work for the town), debited from the treasury, which must hold it (owner decision 37, "all three ways"; it is owed, not conditional).

## Status
OPEN

## Tier
standard

## Type
feature

## Priority
P2

## Request Summary
Decision 37 (the user ruled directly, 2026-10-09; designer milestone branch `milestone-d27-earn-then-buy`) gives the first coin three ways: building tills and stock, merchant trade capital, and faction-treasury wages for town work. Lane B's batch 2 builds the first two plus the INN wage paid from the inn's own purse (decision 34). The treasury wage was deferred as ORDERING only. The designer (2026-10-09) objected to making it conditional, so it is owed regardless of the batch-2 measurement.

Today the faction treasuries are `global_resources` keys (`faction_<id>_gold`, 1000 each). They sit outside entity and building coin accounting; `regional_sovereignty` adds tax to them.

## Scope
1. Design for rpg-planner:
   - what "town work" is (a task at the town hall, upkeep, a public job) and where it is offered;
   - the treasury debit as a typed transfer with a payer (a new source kind?), so conservation covers treasury coin as well;
   - the wage and shift length, by fiction (decision 33);
   - LIQUIDITY_EXHAUSTED when the treasury is short.

   Ask before adding any new field or enum, and check the perf Phase B file list first (state.py, apply.py and others are held during the slot).
2. Build it, with a kernel scenario (a designer spec: town work paid from the treasury; an empty treasury pays nothing) and conservation over the run (treasury plus purses).
3. Measure with a pinned 5x3.

## Out of Scope
- The inn wage (batch 2).
- Making minted coin need a payer (TCK-20261009-MINTED-KILL-AND-QUEST-COIN-IS-PAID-BY-A-PAYER-EXCH-02-B), although it shares the treasury-as-payer accounting. Consider sequencing the two together.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A broke town worker can do town work paid from its town's faction treasury. No coin appears. The treasury balance falls by the wage.
