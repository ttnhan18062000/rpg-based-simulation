# TCK-20261009-SLEEP-DEBT-WEAKENS-THEN-COLLAPSES-THE-SUBJECT-WHERE-IT-STANDS-NO-HP-LOSS

## Title
Past a high sleep-debt line a subject weakens, and at 98 or more it falls asleep where it stands and cannot act until its debt drops below a wake line; it loses no HP (fatigue rule, owner ruling 2026-10-09, replacing Bible 01's +1 HP per tick).

## Status
OPEN

## Tier
standard

## Type
feature

## Priority
P2

## Request Summary
The owner ruled (via rpg-designer) that sleep debt is "weaken, then collapse". Today `apply.py` costs 1 HP per tick at sleep_debt 98 or more: the same shape as the old starvation defect that D36 replaced. The designer writes the kernel spec (thresholds, wake line) and routes the code through rpg-planner. It reuses D36's pattern: `src/engine/starvation.py`, a frozen table; existing hooks such as combat.py's `sleep_debt > 80` gives attack x0.8 (EXHAUSTION).

## Scope
1. A design proposal to rpg-planner: the weakened threshold (reuse 80, the existing EXHAUSTION line?); the collapse at 98 as a forced sleep in place (a typed rest task or state, with no new field without asking); the wake line; how the scheduler treats a collapsed subject (no actions); and the interaction with threats (helpless in place, so attackers get free hits).
2. Remove the HP loss; build; unit tests per stage; the designer's kernel scenario.
3. Measure at 5000 ticks, pinned 5x3: collapse episodes, deaths by cause, alive.

## Out of Scope
- Starvation (D36, done).

## Acceptance Criteria
- No HP loss from sleep debt. A collapsed subject takes no action until below the wake line, and the scenario passes.
- Bible 01, divergence and parity are updated.
