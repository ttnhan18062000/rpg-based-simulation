# TCK-20261009-GENERIC-PLANT-EATER-ARCHETYPES-GRAZE-AND-FLEE-DECISIONS-45-50

## Title
Add one or two GENERIC plant-eater archetypes (e.g. a small plant-eater and a large plant-eater), defined by COARSE property bands (size, power and wits as small/medium/large; plant diet; edible to meat diets), that graze wild land and keep away from or flee danger, so meat-eaters mostly eat game and people have something to hunt (owner decisions 45 and 50; no named species).

## Request Summary
Owner decision 45 (the user decided directly, 2026-10-09; designer branch `milestone-animal-ways`, after commit 680da59cd). The corpus has 13 species and no herbivore, so SURV-06's "grazing creatures forage" has nothing to apply to, and predators have no game. This ticket pairs with hunting (TCK-20261009-A-KILL-LEAVES-MEAT-AND-A-CARNIVORE-EATS-IT-RAW-WITH-A-DIET-GATE-DECISION-44).

## Scope
1. Design for rpg-planner. All of it is content plus existing systems wherever possible:
   - the species (2 or 3 small herbivores), their need profiles (decision 42: their own rates), and their diet (wild plant food: they graze the decision-29 wild food and/or a grass node kind);
   - placement by biome (ENV-08: near and wild land, not settled land);
   - breeding through the existing ECOL population rules. Numbers follow those rules, never a target;
   - fleeing through the existing threat and flight logic (AGENCY-07, decision 32's posture: a deer beside a wolf avoids);
   - their meat yield (by body, used by hunting).
   Watch perf's Phase B file list.
2. Build it, with a designer kernel spec if they write one, and unit tests (a deer grazes, flees a wolf, and breeds within ECOL caps).
3. Measure at 5000 ticks, pinned 5x3, per kind group (decision 42): prey population over time (no explosion or collapse beyond ECOL bounds), predator ever-ate and starvation, and wolf attacks on people (decision 45: they should FALL as game becomes available).

## Out of Scope
- Cooking, and people hunting for sale (later; this only makes it possible).

## Acceptance Criteria
- Prey exists in the worlds that have wild land, grazes, breeds within ECOL bounds, and flees predators.
- Predators feed mostly on game, and wolf attacks on people fall compared with the base.

## Assumptions / Open Questions
- **DECISION 50 (user direct, 2026-10-09):** no named real species (no deer or hare) and no exact figures in content or tests. Use generic archetypes with coarse bands; tests assert on stage order and relations, not tuned numbers. Named species and fine values come after the foundation is hardened. LB-S20 and LB-S21 now name archetypes (designer commit after df827d945).
- **DECISION 46 (user direct, 2026-10-09) BINDS THIS TICKET:** "predator", "prey", "grazer" and "hunter" are NOT labels. A kind declares PROPERTIES only: body size, power or danger, wits, diet (what it can eat) and edibility (which diets its body feeds). Hunting is DERIVED: the subject is hungry, its diet can eat the other's body, and it can overcome the other (reuse the combat_engagement posture verdict and the KNOW-04 danger prior). Fleeing is DERIVED from perceived danger. No species-level prey or predator flag may exist, and no behaviour may read one. The diet gate stays (it IS a property). The specs gained a derivation arm (LB-S20: a hungry wolf beside a larger, dangerous edible creature does not hunt it, and no flag is read) and a no-danger control (LB-S21: a deer beside a similar plant-eater keeps grazing). Also check LB-S15's individual `ecological_predator` role: if behaviour reads it as a fixed label, it is CONFLICTING.
