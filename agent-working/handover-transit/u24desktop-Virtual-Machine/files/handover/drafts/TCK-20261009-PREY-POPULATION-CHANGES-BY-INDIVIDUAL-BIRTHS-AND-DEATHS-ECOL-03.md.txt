## Request Summary
ECOL-03 is a standing finding: `DemographicCycleService.process_demographics` (`domains/demographics/cohort.py:349`) changes populations statistically, so LB-S21 part 3 (a newborn only from a living, fed adult; no respawn; every death has a cause; zero adults means it stays zero) cannot pass. This was split out of the prey ticket (TCK-20261009-SMALL-PREY-KINDS-...-DECISION-45) on 2026-10-09.

## Scope
1. Design for rpg-planner: the birth eligibility per adult (fed, mature, with an interval), where the per-adult last-birth or fertility record lives (an existing field if possible; any state.py field needs rpg-planner's yes, and it lands after perf's Phase B), the litter as content, and how the aggregate reads individual events.
2. Build it, with LB-S21 part 3 as a kernel test.
3. Measure the populations per kind over 5000 and 10000 ticks (no explosion or collapse beyond ECOL bounds).
