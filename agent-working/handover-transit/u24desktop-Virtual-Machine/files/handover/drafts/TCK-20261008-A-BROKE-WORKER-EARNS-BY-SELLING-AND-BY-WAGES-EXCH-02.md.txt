# TCK-20261008-A-BROKE-WORKER-EARNS-BY-SELLING-AND-BY-WAGES-EXCH-02

## Title
A broke worker earns coin by selling what it gathered, hunted, made or looted, and by a wage for work at a shop, inn or smithy, each paid from the payer's own purse (EXCH-02, owner decision 34).

## Status

## Request Summary
Owner decision 34 (the user decided directly, 2026-10-08; designer milestone branch `milestone-d27-earn-then-buy`, commit 92f43c41e): new Rule EXCH-02. A broke person earns by (a) SELLING goods to a buyer, (b) a WAGE for work at a shop, inn or smithy, or (c) a task or quest reward. Every coin comes out of the payer's OWN purse, which must hold it. No money comes from need, and there is no starting purse.

Today (Lane B's feasibility read): all 14 town_council workers start with 0 gold, and gold inflow over a run is 5 to 40 gold in total. `opening_steps._earn_then_buy_step` is a stub. `ShopService.sell_item` and SHOP_SELL exist, but nobody sells. Wages are MISSING. Shops sell any registry item through `buy_item`, but nothing buys in a live run, because the shop and blacksmith paths never run in a compiled world (TCK-20261007-SHOP-AND-BLACKSMITH-..., `state.building_tiles` is empty).

This is the earning that unblocks the parked free-meal removal (TCK-20261007-EAT-BESIDE-THE-INN-FEEDS-A-SUBJECT-THAT-CANNOT-PAY-FREE-MEAL) in all three measured worlds. urban_political has no wild land by design (decision 35), so its people must earn by a WAGE (or a task).

## Scope
1. SELL first: the earn opening step offers selling carried goods (gathered, looted, made) to a shop whose purse holds the price. The shop pays from its own purse; conservation of gold and items holds (Bible 03). Fix the shop lookup through `service_reach` (absorbing TCK-20261007-SHOP-AND-BLACKSMITH-TOWN-PATHS-NEVER-RAN-IN-A-COMPILED-WORLD, which rides the same batch).
2. PRICE LAW (rpg-planner ruling): `MarketSystem.calculate_price` is authoritative (Bible 03, Cost). Route `ShopService.buy_item` (and the sell price, if it differs) through it. If MarketSystem's buy factor is missing from the Bible 03 text, add it, with a parity entry.
3. WAGE: a work action at a shop, inn or smithy, paid per shift from the employer building's or owner's purse. Propose the shape to rpg-planner BEFORE building: who owns the purse, how a shift is modeled (a held task with a duration), the wage amount (content, with a fiction reason, under decision 33), and which state, if any, is new (ask before any state.py or apply.py field).
4. Task and quest rewards: report whether the existing quest system pays coin from a payer's purse to a town worker in a compiled world. No build unless it is a small wiring fix.
5. Scenarios (the designer owes the specs): sell gathered food, the shop purse falls, buy a meal; work a shift at the inn, paid from the inn's purse, eat.

## Out of Scope
- Paid small jobs between townsfolk (social contracts, later).
- The blacksmith wholesale recipe learning, unless the shop fix forces it on (then report it).

## Acceptance Criteria
- In each measured world, a broke worker earns by at least one EXCH-02 path and then buys food (occurrence counts per run, pinned 5x3).
- No coin is created: every payment is debited from a payer that holds it (conservation tests).
- The kernel scenarios with control arms pass.
- Divergence, parity, Bible 03 and the mechanisms.yaml note (market/shop entry) are written.
