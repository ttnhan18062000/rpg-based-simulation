# TCK-20261008-A-HELD-REGROUP-MOVE-WALKS-INTO-PERCEIVED-HOSTILES-AND-IS-NEVER-RE-DECIDED

## Title
A held REGROUP move, chosen when no hostile was perceived, keeps walking after hostiles appear and takes opportunity attacks, because the next brain tick is up to 10 ticks away.

## Status
OPEN

## Tier
standard

## Type
bug

## Priority
P1

## Request Summary
This is the residual held-move class after the leftover-navigation-target fix (branch rpg-oa-neither-fix). Lane A's urban_political attribution (pinned, seeds 42-46, main 10944422c vs the fix):
- DEFEAT deaths caused by held moves rose from 11 to 30, of which REGROUP went from 4 to 20.
- Held-move opportunity-attack hits by reason went from REGROUP 33 to 147, PURSUE 48 to 71, PANIC_RETREAT 9 to 26.
- Most of it is on seed 45: DEFEAT 5 to 19, held-move hits 32 to 123.

REGROUP is emitted in the no-hostiles branch (`src/engine/tactical.py` about 412-425) for a subject outside its group's cohesion radius. It is a held ENTITY_MOVE, so it is not re-decided until the next brain tick. The live NORMAL policy runs `strategic_intelligence = 10` (`policy.py:92`).

Hypothesis (unverified, Lane A): subjects that used to stay locked in an ATTACK loop now finish the fight, get sent back to their group anchor, and walk into hostiles that reappear.

The earlier held-move interrupt (WIP 7f2455353) did not help, because the brain it handed to was up to 10 ticks away. Any fix must account for that.

## Scope
1. A read-only trace of the REGROUP deaths on seed 45 one by one: the tick REGROUP was decided, when a hostile became perceived, how many steps were taken while the hostile was perceived, and the damage source. Confirm or refute the hypothesis.
2. Propose (do not build) the fix shape to rpg-planner. Candidates:
   - a held move that is not a tracked combat move ends when a perceived catalog-hostile is adjacent, and the subject holds (a typed hold, as CONFLICT-04 does) instead of waiting for the brain;
   - REGROUP is not emitted, or is cut short, while a hostile is within perception.
   Bringing the brain forward is a governor/policy change (perf, #415) and is not in scope.
3. Implement the ruled fix, with tests and a pinned 5-seed x 3-world report.

## Out of Scope
- The scheduler, policy and cadence (perf #415).
- CONFLICT-04 (hold between blows): a separate ticket that lands first.

## Assumptions / Open Questions
- rpg-designer (2026-10-08, finding): the existing Rules cover this, so no new ruling is needed. A held move is a commitment, not a guarantee (AGENCY-01), and under CONFLICT-04 a step beside or away from a hostile is a decision. A held REGROUP that meets a perceived hostile is re-decided (the same pattern as OUT_OF_RANGE and the dry-node fix), and then the existing gates apply: fight, flee under AGENCY-07, or route around.
- It goes back to the designer only if the fix lets a regroup outrank or override a present threat, or lets group cohesion decide flight (AGENCY-02/07 meaning).
