# TCK-20261009-A-KILL-LEAVES-MEAT-AND-A-CARNIVORE-EATS-IT-RAW-WITH-A-DIET-GATE-DECISION-44

## Title
Hunting: a kill leaves a meat item, a carnivore eats it raw (raw fills less, decision 31), and a diet gate lets each kind eat only what its diet allows, so carnivores stop foraging berries (owner decision 44).

## Status
OPEN

## Tier

## Request Summary
Owner decision 44 (the user decided directly, 2026-10-09; designer branch `milestone-animal-ways`, commit 7ba43c48c): the free-meal removal WAITS for the animals' own ways, then lands for every kind at once. Lane B's read found:
- no meat, carcass or hide item;
- kill rewards pay gold and XP only, and corpses hold loot, not food;
- no eat-corpse action;
- carnivores (wolf, spider; carnivore_survival) eat only by accident, by walking to a town inn for the free relief or by foraging a berry thicket.

There are no grazers in the corpus.

## Scope
1. A design proposal to rpg-planner before building. It must cover:
   - a `raw_meat` item, with a raw fill below the cooked value (decision 31: raw fills less);
   - how a kill leaves it: the corpse inventory, sized by the prey's body (content);
   - a hunt opening step for carnivore kinds: choose prey, engage, then eat from the corpse or carry the meat;
   - a DIET field per species or need profile (content) gating EAT and forage: carnivores meat only, people omnivore, and so on.

   Watch the perf Phase B file list (state.py, apply.py, executor.py, domain_logic.py and others are held during the slot): say if the kill-drop needs a held file. Ask before any new field or enum in state.py or apply.py.
2. Build it, with the designer's kernel spec (a wolf kills prey, the carcass yields meat, the wolf eats it raw and its hunger falls; the control kills nothing) and unit tests for the diet gate (a wolf never forages berries; a person can eat both).
3. Measure at 5000 ticks, pinned 5x3, PER KIND GROUP (decision 42): wildlife ever-ate, starvation and alive; also prey deaths, since hunting raises predation.

## Out of Scope
- Cooking, fire and the hearth (later).
- Grazers (none exist).
- The free-meal removal itself (parked; it lands after this, for all kinds at once).

## Acceptance Criteria
- In living_world, carnivores feed by hunting, and the diet gate means 0 carnivore berry forages and 0 carnivore inn meals.
- Conservation: meat enters only by a kill and leaves only by eating or decay (if decay is modelled).

## Assumptions / Open Questions
- **DECISION 46 (user direct, 2026-10-09) BINDS THIS TICKET:** "predator", "prey", "grazer" and "hunter" are NOT labels. A kind declares PROPERTIES only: body size, power or danger, wits, diet (what it can eat) and edibility (which diets its body feeds). Hunting is DERIVED: the subject is hungry, its diet can eat the other's body, and it can overcome the other (reuse the combat_engagement posture verdict and the KNOW-04 danger prior). Fleeing is DERIVED from perceived danger. No species-level prey or predator flag may exist, and no behaviour may read one. The diet gate stays (it IS a property). The specs gained a derivation arm (LB-S20: a hungry wolf beside a larger, dangerous edible creature does not hunt it, and no flag is read) and a no-danger control (LB-S21: a deer beside a similar plant-eater keeps grazing). Also check LB-S15's individual `ecological_predator` role: if behaviour reads it as a fixed label, it is CONFLICTING.
