When a fix encodes **what something means in the world** — what a raid targets, what "safety" is, what
counts as a legitimate destination, owner, or cause — get `world-rule-catalog-design`'s read **before
writing code**. Accepted Rule IDs outrank my judgement
([[reference_world_rules_catalog_governs_sim_semantics]]), so implementing first risks building a fix
that encodes a wrong rule and then citing it as evidence later.

**Confirmed 2026-10-03, the global raid.** I measured a real defect (`check_for_raid` spawned every
raider on a radius-25 ring around hardcoded `(0,0)`, 12/12 outside every region, inert — it had never
produced a raid), designed a fix (anchor on a `PlaceKind.CITY`, else a region's geometric centre),
implemented it, unit-tested it green, committed it — **and only then asked.** The answer withdrew it:
- **PLACE-01** says a Place is a declared subject "rather than remaining mere terrain or a bare
  coordinate", so my region-centre fallback invented a destination the rule layer excludes.
- **CAUSE-01 / ID-04 / ORG-03** require a raid to have a real causal and organizational origin; a world
  clock is not one. The right answer was **retire the mechanic**, not re-anchor it.
- Re-anchoring would have *activated* a behaviour the world never had — an Intentional Gameplay Change
  parked by row 7 — so my `Bug Fix` rationale class was wrong too.

Two measured facts also showed the fix wouldn't even have worked: 11 of 12 re-anchored spawns were
**still** off-region (the ring radius exceeds the region half-width), and `tactical.py:141`
`PANIC_RETREAT` overwrites the target with the same `(0,0)` within 15-38 ticks.

**Why:** the cost is asymmetric. Asking first costs one message; asking after cost an implementation, a
test suite, a divergence entry, a parity edit, and a full rewrite — plus the risk that a wrong rule
ships and is cited as precedent. The rule owner answered in one exchange both times.

**How to apply:** before implementing anything semantic, send the question with the measurement attached
and the specific decision named, and say explicitly that I will rewrite if the direction is wrong.
Follow-up tickets should carry this as an AC — `TCK-20261003-TACTICAL-RETREAT-TARGETS-HARDCODED-WORLD-ORIGIN`
AC-6 requires the rule-layer question be put **before** implementation, which is the corrected order.
Raise my own doubts against my own work: flagging that the region-centre fallback was the part I was
least comfortable with is what made the "no" fast and cheap. Relates to
[[feedback_options_and_recommendation_on_concerns]].
