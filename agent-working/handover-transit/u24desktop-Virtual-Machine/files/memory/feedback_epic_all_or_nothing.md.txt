Don't cherry-pick individual clean tickets out of an epic folder when some of its sibling tickets conflict with active work. Treat the epic as one all-or-nothing unit: either every child ticket is currently doable, or the whole epic waits.

**Why:** User corrected this directly after I recommended doing 2 of 6 tickets in `cognition-graph-analytics-pipeline` (the other 4 touched files actively being edited by the RPG-core branch). Partial completion leaves an epic permanently half-open in a confusing state and doesn't match how epics are meant to be tracked/closed (see `## Related Tickets`/epic-closing rule in CLAUDE.md: "This epic is not closed until its child tickets ... reach tickets/done/").

**How to apply:** During a standalone-ticket survey (see [[project_session_role_division]]), when checking an epic folder in `tickets/todos/{folder}/` for conflicts against active worktree branches, if ANY child ticket in that folder has a real file-level conflict with an active branch, exclude the ENTIRE folder from the candidate list -- do not offer a partial/opportunistic subset as an option, even if some individual tickets are clean and dependency-free. Only recommend an epic folder when 100% of its child tickets are conflict-free.
