Don't "fix" a low or missing SimQ pillar score by making the scorer/selection logic favor an
action entities wouldn't realistically choose (e.g. cranking up `craft_upgrade`/`buy_upgrade`
route scores until entities pick them, regardless of whether they have a legible need/opportunity
to do so).

**Why:** this is a realistic RPG simulation, not a metrics-optimization target. The user's exact
framing (2026-08-05): "if entity need to perform harvest, it must be a reasonable route (cognition,
action, etc)". A forced route trades one artificial signal (a pillar's silence) for another
(unmotivated behavior) — it looks like progress in the score but isn't real quality improvement.

**How to apply:** before proposing any scoring/selection fix for a SimQ gap, first determine
whether the zero/low signal is a genuine miscalibration or bug, versus a correct reflection that
the corpus's entities/situations don't actually warrant that behavior. Structure such tickets as
investigation-first, with an acceptance criterion that any fix be justified by a specific,
named cognitive/action-layer scenario where the behavior should legitimately have won — not a
blanket weight/bias bump. See `TCK-20260805-SIMQ-ECONOMY-ADVENTURE-ROUTE-SCORER-BIAS` for the
concrete example this came from (AdventureRouteScorer never selecting craft/buy routes).
