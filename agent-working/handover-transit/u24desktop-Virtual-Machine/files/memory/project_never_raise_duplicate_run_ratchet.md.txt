`tools/gate_checks/duplicate_run_record_check.py`'s `IDENTICAL_OUTCOME_CEILING` constant (currently
`1`) is a ratchet, not a budget: its own comment says "May only decrease. Raising it to paper over
a newly-introduced accidental duplicate defeats the entire point of this check." rpg-feature-planning
explicitly confirmed and reinforced this 2026-09-29 when I asked whether to raise it after
accidentally introducing a duplicate `runs.jsonl` row — the answer was no, remove the duplicate row
instead. See [[feedback_never_reuse_end_ts_across_record_run_calls]] for the concrete mistake shape
that produces this situation, and how to fix it correctly (delete the stale uncommitted line, never
touch a pushed/merged row).

**Why:** raising the ceiling is the "my case is special" failure mode the constant exists to resist
— it always feels locally reasonable in the moment (a legitimate-looking correction call happened
to produce this shape) but structurally indistinguishable from ignoring a genuine accidental
duplicate write.

**How to apply:** if `duplicate_run_record_check.py` or its companion pytest
(`tests/tools/test_duplicate_run_record_check.py::test_real_corpus_is_at_or_below_the_ratchet_ceiling`)
fails with a new identical-outcome group, first identify whether it's a still-uncommitted mistake of
your own (fix by deleting the stale line) before ever considering the ceiling itself negotiable —
and even then, don't decide unilaterally; this is exactly the kind of call that goes back to the
ticket owner.
