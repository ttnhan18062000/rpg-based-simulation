A `verdict:`/`verified.verdict:` field in `registries/mechanisms.yaml` records what some earlier run
found. It is **not** standing evidence, and it is not re-checked when a ticket cites it.

**Confirmed 2026-09-30.** `TCK-20260920-DEMOGRAPHIC-COHORT-CYCLE-POPULATION-COHORTS-NEVER-SEEDED` was
filed on 2026-09-20 on the strength of a `mechanisms.yaml` verdict dated 2026-09-16. Its central
premise — "no world ever seeds `population_cohorts`" — was **already false on the day it was filed**:
`TCK-20260831-POPULATION-COHORT-SEEDING` had shipped the seeding three weeks earlier
(`src/worldbuilding/compiler.py:449`, unconditional).

Verified independently by compiling `frontier_living_world` end-to-end: all 6 `spawn_region` values
match a real `RegionSpec.id`, zero mismatches, 6 of 7 regions seeded (hometown 13 →
{young 4, adult 6, elder 3}, exact 30/50/20 largest-remainder split); the 7th has no `PopulationSpec`
at all, which is documented zero-population behaviour.

**How to apply:**
- Before filing or grouping a ticket that cites a `mechanisms.yaml` verdict, re-verify the claim
  against code, and date both the verdict and the last commit to the cited code.
- `STALE-PREMISE` is the label for this outcome — the premise was false when filed. It is a
  **ticket-provenance** finding, not a mechanism verdict, and routes to `agent-working-design`.
- A resolved seeding claim does **not** certify the mechanism fires. That is a separate question and
  forcing it into the same verdict is the collapse [[project_m2_temporal_axis_gap_pending]] warns
  about.
- Expect more of these among the remaining unreachable-mechanism corpus tickets; several cite
  2026-09-16 registry verdicts.
