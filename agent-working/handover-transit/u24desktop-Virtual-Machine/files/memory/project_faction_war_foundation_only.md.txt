Faction war is a large, complex feature and the user has deliberately capped investment in it:
**build a bit of foundation, then work on the lower features that support it.** Do not keep pushing
the war chain upward.

Stated 2026-09-15, after faction sentiment shipped (pairwise tension, sentiment accumulating from
real interaction, TENSE/HOSTILE reachable).

**What was queued and is now deprioritised:** the military-strength driver (loop #1,
`military_strength` frozen at 1.0), and the faction importance signal
(`TCK-20260915-FACTION-IMPORTANCE-SIGNAL-INITIATIVE`). Both remain real and filed; neither is next.

**What "lower features that support it" means concretely** — the systems that produce the inputs
war needs, all currently starved:
- cross-faction combat rarity (zero in 3 of 4 corpus worlds)
- regional trauma never accumulating (lair regions never see combat)
- calamity intensity never firing (sole producer never runs)
- regional influence shift never firing

**Why:** these are the "a real system fed by nothing" family — mechanisms that work perfectly and
receive no input. They sit *below* faction politics and feed it, so fixing them is both
higher-priority under [[feedback-bottom-up-rpg-feature-priority]] and the actual precondition for
war ever being worth building.
