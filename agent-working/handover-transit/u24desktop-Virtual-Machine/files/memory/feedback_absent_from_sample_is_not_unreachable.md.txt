Do not reason from "I did not observe X in my sample" to "X cannot occur". State the
observation and the mechanism separately, and check the code path for whether anything
*prevents* X before excluding it.

**Why:** on 2026-10-01 (C1, `TCK-20260928-ENTITY-DEATH-AUTHORITY-BOUNDARY-CHECK`) I made five
substantive errors in one session and every one was caught by a peer, not by me. Four shared this
single root:

1. Advised keying a death branch on "`alive=False` with no `death_reason`" — invisible for passive
   deaths, because `apply.py:106/109` sets `alive=False` *and* `active=False` in the same apply and
   the loop guard then skips the entity forever. I generalised from a corpus sample that contained
   no passive deaths.
2. Grouped 9 production rows under one "unrecorded death" heading when they were **two opposite
   defects** sharing one committed signature (deaths not recorded vs. non-deaths becoming deaths).
3. Claimed `EntityRole.HERO` was an exact discriminant for `DEFEAT`/`REBIRTH`. False — both
   opportunity-attack call sites force `is_lethal=False` for *any* role. I had seen zero
   opportunity-attack `DEFEAT`s in 120 ticks and concluded the path away.
4. Relayed a decision to ship a `HAZARD` classification without noticing it inherits the same
   unpersisted-cause problem one layer up (`world_dynamics.py:39` clobbers the outcome before
   `resolve_lifecycle` reads it). A defect reached the branch because of this.

The irony worth remembering: I had written exactly this caveat into C1's own investigation
("this is not evidence of unreachability — it is the expected outcome of sampling a conjunction of
two rare events") and then violated it twice within the hour.

**How to apply:** when a measurement comes back empty, write "not observed in N ticks under
conditions C" — never "unreachable", "cannot", or "only ever". Before claiming a discriminant is
exact, grep every call site of the thing it discriminates on. When relaying someone else's
decision, re-derive whether it still holds against the code, because relaying launders my own
unchecked assumption into their work. See [[feedback-a-probe-is-an-instrument-that-can-be-wrong]]
and [[feedback-check-recorded-decision-before-filing-defect]].
