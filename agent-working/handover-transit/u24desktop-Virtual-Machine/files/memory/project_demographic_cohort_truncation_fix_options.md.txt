`TCK-20260930-DEMOGRAPHIC-COHORT-NET-DELTA-TRUNCATES-TO-ZERO` (on `origin/main`, `tickets/todos/`)
has an **open fix-direction design call** as of 2026-10-01. `DemographicCycleService` computes
`net = int(count * 0.01)`, so `net >= 1` needs `count >= 100`; the largest seeded cohort bracket in
the 9-world corpus is 6. Fix site `src/domains/demographics/cohort.py:377-381`.

Three directions, not one:
1. **Fractional accumulator** — carry the discarded remainder. Preserves the declared rate semantics
   exactly. **Cost: new durable state**, so under the repo's Durable State Rule it needs a typed
   model, a stable location in world state, a defined lifecycle, inspection/debug visibility and
   tests. A real architectural commitment, not a one-line fix.
2. **Rate re-basing** — a balance change, not a bug fix, and it makes a declared rate's meaning
   depend on world size.
3. **Seeded stochastic rounding** through the existing domain RNG (`rng.get_int(Domain...)`) —
   preserves expected value, stays deterministic by seed, adds **no durable state**. **This is the
   option nobody had named**, and may well be the answer.

**Why:** `test-architecture-implementer` asked me to pick a direction so its pipeline exercise could
reach Implement. I declined — deciding in five minutes to unblock someone else's test would have put
a snap judgement in the ticket wearing the authority of a considered one, and no later reader could
tell which it was. A pipeline that detects an undecided design and stops with `NEEDS_HUMAN_INPUT` is
demonstrating the behaviour being verified, not failing. The user was offered the scoping and has not
prioritised it.

**How to apply:** this is still mine (`rpg-feature-planning`) to decide properly, with scoping, not
in passing. Evaluate (3) first. If (1) wins, budget for the durable-state obligations up front.
