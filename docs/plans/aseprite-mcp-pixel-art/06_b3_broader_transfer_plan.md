# Milestone 06 — B3 Broader Experimental Transfer

## Status and outcome

**Dormant, conditional, and optional.** Determine whether an adaptive result proven in one narrow task can
transfer across selected asset families or contexts. B3 does not create a production pipeline or permit a
project-wide claim from one identity, reviewer, size, palette, display context, or experiment family.

## Prerequisites

- B2 full `CAP-B01`–`CAP-B09 PASS` for its declared scope and explicit human authorization for one transfer question.
- New frozen transfer/held-out registry whose expected solutions, close siblings, lessons, and outcomes were
  unavailable during prior retrieval and curation.
- Named human reviewers, effect/non-regression thresholds, budgets, and stopping rule.
- Selected target family comes from existing `ART-W01`–`ART-W07`; no roster expansion through this plan.

## Deliverables

| ID | Deliverable | Objective acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| `B3-W01` | Transfer charter | Names source scope, one target scope, causal hypothesis, prohibited inference, and contamination boundary |
| `B3-W02` | Scope mapping | Identifies which guidance is reusable, narrowed, incompatible, or excluded without project-wide defaults |
| `B3-W03` | Independent evaluation packet | Frozen fixtures, neutral labels, equal budgets, blind review, score visibility, and complete provenance |
| `B3-W04` | Drift/security rerun | Scope isolation, epoch, injection, promotion, quarantine, and rollback gates repeated for the new context |
| `B3-W05` | Transfer disposition | Retain source-only scope, approve target-specific scope, request evidence, or reject transfer |

## Non-goals

- Universal pixel-art rules, final style guide, automated production, or broad roster generation.
- Changing final resolution, palette, style, status/skill roster, Place representation, faction emblems, animation scope, or pipeline.
- Making B3 mandatory for supervised M1 or narrow B2 use.
- Inferring project preference from repeated choices by one reviewer.

## Authorization boundary

Only the named transfer question and frozen target set are authorized after a later approval. Existing rules
remain scoped unless a human explicitly approves a new exact version after target evidence. No automated
scope broadening, inheritance, or production activation exists.

## Covered capability gates

B3 reruns applicable `CAP-B02`–`CAP-B09` checks for scope transfer. It adds no new core `CAP-A` claim and
does not convert experimental `CAP-B PASS` into production readiness.

## Required evidence and security checks

- Independent source/target split, freeze/hash, assignment/unblinding dates, and contamination audit.
- Included/excluded guidance and cases, scope-match decisions, conflicts, model/retriever epoch, and full review order.
- Primary practical transfer outcome plus all reliability, readability, constraint, and invariant non-regressions.
- Repeat injection, impersonation, approval replay, scorer anchoring, deletion/revocation, and rollback checks
  in the new scope.

## Objective exit criteria

`PASS` means one named target scope meets the preregistered held-out effect and every non-regression/security
condition. It authorizes at most a target-specific approved guidance version. Insufficient breadth or sample
is `INCONCLUSIVE`; regression, contamination, unsafe scope broadening, or authority failure is `FAIL`.

## Dependencies

B3 depends on B2 and on M2 when the target outcome is native-display readability. Every new transfer target
is a separate charter; targets do not depend on each other.

## Rollback path

Retire or quarantine only the target-scope guidance/index epoch, restore the last proven narrow B2 snapshot,
retain negative transfer evidence, and leave M1/no-memory operation available.

## Stop conditions

Stop after the first failed or inconclusive transfer unless a human approves one procedural rerun. Stop if
the target corpus was exposed earlier, reviewer independence is inadequate, scope cannot be typed, or a
broader claim would require freezing art direction.

## Ticket-ready slices after activation

One transfer charter/split; scope mapping; independent evaluation; threat/rollback rerun; human disposition.
Never pre-create a generic “project-wide adaptive art” ticket.
