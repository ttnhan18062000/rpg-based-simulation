# Final Replacement Boundary Summary

This document defines the authoritative boundary between supported replacement and unsupported remainder as of Phase 11 close.

## 1. Supported Replacement Boundary (RATIFIED)

The following behaviors are officially replaced by `src` and backed by bit-identical or contractual proof:

- **Movement Substrate**: All Manhattan/Cardinal movement, pathfinding, and congestion sidestepping.
- **Resource/Interaction**: All harvesting, looting, channeled states, and inventory capacity limits.
- **Strategic Cognition**: Bounded project selection, lead interpretation, and strategic redirection.
- **Social Trust**: Trust-weighted recruitment, betrayal memory, and social appraisal.
- **Legality Enforcement**: Line-of-Sight, engagement-registry truth, and move rejection.
- **System Compatibility**: CLI entry, JSON-L replay, structured logging, and REST/WebSocket protocols.

## 2. Divergent-but-Supported Boundary (RATIFIED)

The following behaviors diverge from legacy but are officially supported as improved standards:

- **Bounded Cognition**: AI attention and detour limits (replacing legacy recursion bugs).
- **Hardened Invariants**: Engagement-aware move legality (replacing legacy geometry exploits).
- **Normalization**: Lowercase registry keys and normalized API schemas.

## 3. Unsupported Remainder (EXCLUDED)

The following behaviors are **NOT** replaced and must not be assumed during Phase 12 cutover:

- **Turning Points**: Deferred.
- **Economy Overhaul**: Survival needs, complex breakthrough math, and territory ownership deferred.
- **Narrative Events**: Randomized event generators deferred.
