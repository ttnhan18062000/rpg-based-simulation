Open Ideation: What Would You Build?

This is a creative-generation task, not a review. You're being given a
fantasy RPG simulation's creative direction and a light sense of what it
already delivers, then asked one open question: given all this, what
would you actually build next?

Deliberately, you are not being handed a list of ideas the project has
already had. That's on purpose. The point of asking someone outside this
project is to get a genuinely independent read, not a set of ideas shaped
by trying to guess or avoid what's already been thought of internally.
Don't self-censor for overlap. Propose whatever you actually believe in.
Filtering for duplicates, fit, and feasibility happens afterward, on our
side, with full knowledge of the project's history -- not something you
need to do or guess at here.

Read this first
-------------------
The Unwritten World -- the creative direction:
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/4dcbe5f7-4cea-467e-aaf0-794ee5dc7e3f

Read it and take it seriously -- it's eight principles describing what
this specific world is trying to be, each with real examples and an
explicit "what failure looks like." This is the actual compass. An idea
that's "a cool RPG feature" in the abstract but doesn't fit what this
particular world is going for probably belongs to a different project.
You don't need to force-fit every idea to a named principle, but the best
ones usually will, and it's worth saying which if it's clear.

Optional, for light orientation only
-----------------------------------------
Simulation Capabilities -- what already exists:
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/72b90756-e335-46ce-a2be-9ab757357bd0

A plain-language summary of what the simulation currently delivers, live
today versus built-but-inactive versus not yet built. Useful for a general
sense of scale and texture -- one character up through parties, cities,
nations, peoples, and the world. Not a checklist to avoid. If an idea you
believe in turns out to already exist in some form, propose it anyway and
say so if you happen to notice -- that's still useful information, and
it's not your job to hunt for overlap.

What to send back
----------------------
As many ideas as you genuinely believe in -- one exceptional one is worth
more than ten mediocre ones, but there's no target count either way. Any
scale: one character, a settlement, a nation, a people, the world itself,
or something that starts small and ripples outward.

For each idea:
- A short name.
- A paragraph describing what it is and what it would actually change
  about how the world behaves or what stories it can produce.
- Which of the eight principles it serves, if that's clear to you -- and
  if it doesn't clearly serve any of them, say why you think it's still
  worth considering.

Plain language throughout -- no code, no implementation detail, no file
paths. Describe it the way you'd describe it to someone who will never
open the source code.
