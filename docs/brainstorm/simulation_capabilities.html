Simulation Capability Overview

What Our Simulation Delivers

A plain-language look at what the simulation engine can actually do today — from a single character's choices, up through parties, cities, nations, and peoples, to the living world around all of them. No code, no technical explanation of how anything works — just what's here, what it does in play, and how far along it is.

2026-08-29 review note. The build order behind "Family, Marriage & Growing Up" and "Goblin Camps, Monster Dens & Ruins" below changed in the planning docs (Marriage no longer gates Reproduction; the Region/Place foundation behind camps/dens/ruins was formally accepted) — see docs/plans/rpg_design_roadmap/rpg_design_roadmap.md for the current dependency picture and its new "Temporal axis" section for a brainstorm-stage proposal about how time itself should work across a character's life. Neither changes the status badges on this page yet — both are still planned, not built.

The short version, before the detail. 14 sections is a lot to read end to end — this table is the whole thing at a glance. Each row links to its section below for the full picture.

AreaStatusIn short
What Characters DoMostly liveCombat and movement are deep and real; a few things (paying for information, direct trading between characters) don't exist yet.
Who Each Character IsMixedStats, race, and personality are all real; two characters of the same class currently grow along an identical path.
Injury & Physical ConditionsLiveWounds and scars are real and lasting; temporary conditions exist but aren't one unified system yet.
How Characters Think & RememberMixed, richest sectionPerception, goals, and relationships are genuinely deep; several fully-built systems (self-perception, emotions, richer memory) are switched off.
Family, Marriage & Growing UpPlannedA complete, thought-through design for birth, marriage, and growing up — none of it built yet.
Working Together: PartiesLiveForming and losing a party both work today, including a real accept-or-refuse decision on joining.
Cities & SettlementsMostly liveReal settlements with buildings and services; the game doesn't always correctly recognize when a character has actually reached one.
The Wider LandMixedTerritory and war damage are real; the population-growth system is built but confirmed to never actually run.
Goblin Camps, Monster Dens & RuinsMostly plannedA real goblin-camp system exists but never starts; most of this section is new design for it and its neighbors.
Nations & PoliticsMixedDiplomacy, reputation, and a real day-to-day ambition system are all live; a nation can never be founded or wiped out entirely.
Organizations Beyond BordersPlannedConfirmed to not exist at all — no organization spanning more than one city or nation yet.
Peoples & RacesMixedA character's own race matters and is real; nothing tracks a whole people's population or general standing yet.
The Living WorldMixedOngoing campaigns and automatic history-writing are both real and substantial; there's no religion system of any kind.
Memory, Reputation & LegacyPlannedNone of this exists yet, but it's a real, thought-through set of ideas for how reputation, feuds, and history could outlive the person who earned them.
Trade, Gear & CraftingMixedGear and the economy are both deep; crafting's rules are real but there's very little to actually craft yet.
