## Notes

**Scope of this regeneration.** This report was regenerated after rebuilding the agent-monitoring
SQLite index (`make agent-monitoring-index`) to avoid the stale-index under-reporting bug flagged in
the prior W33 retro pass — this run reflects 36 total runs / 359 events, up from the 32/315 seen
earlier in the week, now including the 4 tickets closed in this session
(`TCK-20260811-HARVEST-CRAFT-EVENT-DERIVATION-REGRESSION`,
`TCK-20260811-PUSH-EVENT-SHAPERS-DEFAULT-DEV002-VIOLATION`,
`TCK-20260811-SIMQ-COGNITION-BAND-CROSSING-DA-AND-LIFECYCLE-ANCHOR-GAP`,
`TCK-20260812-COMMITTED-INTENTION-SEQUENCE`). `make agent-monitoring-validate` shows zero integrity
warnings for any of these 4 — all pre-existing warnings are historical tickets from June/July.

**What failed most.** `architecture-reviewer` remains the dominant source of gate friction this week:
39% NEEDS_CHANGES at the agent level (23/59 calls) and 40% at the phase level (14/35 Review calls),
consistent with the pattern the earlier W33 retro pass already flagged. The reason-code breakdown
(`dod_condition_failed`: 14, `architecture_violation`: 3, `documentation_accuracy`: 1) shows most
gate friction is Verify/done-checker catching genuinely unmet Definition-of-Done conditions rather
than architecture-reviewer rejecting plans outright — in this session's own 4 tickets, every single
NEEDS_CHANGES/DOD_BLOCKED verdict was a real, evidence-backed finding (a deferred-doc-gap that lacked
a follow-up ticket reference, a doc/code mismatch describing a test guard incorrectly, a stale
event-derivation prose claim in a parity ledger) that got fixed with real substance on re-pass, never
routed around — the high rate reflects genuine rigor, not noise. The `strategy` tag cluster is the
worst-performing tag this week (8 runs, 62% DONE, 3 gate failures) — concentrated in the
adventure-cognition-merge epic and its churn-heavy follow-ups (`TCK-20260810-COGNITION-PROFILE-
ADVENTURE-ELIGIBILITY` alone shows 4 separate run entries at varying durations/statuses, suggesting
multiple resume/retry cycles on one ticket).

**What was slow.** `TCK-20260811-SIMQ-COGNITION-BAND-CROSSING-DA-AND-LIFECYCLE-ANCHOR-GAP` is both
the top duration outlier (7.1x standard-tier median, 630 min) and the top cost-proxy outlier (15.5x
Implement-phase median). This is a session/infrastructure artifact, not a process problem: the
dispatched `implementer` sub-agent stalled three separate times across resumes on a long-running
full-file `-m slow` sequential pytest sweep without completing its own write-up, and the session was
independently interrupted twice by the API rate/session limit resetting mid-ticket. The orchestrating
session ultimately took over Step 9's verification and the ticket write-up directly rather than
continuing to resume the stuck agent. Worth flagging for future retros so this outlier isn't misread
as evidence that the SimQ recalibration work itself is unusually expensive — the actual gate/review
work for that ticket (3 architecture-reviewer passes, all independently re-verified against live
source and fixture files) was proportionate to its real scope.

**What to change.** Search-Before-Grep compliance sits at 81.8% (18/22 Investigate-phase calls) —
4 Investigate dispatches this week did not call `search_docs`/`graphify query` before grep/file reads,
a direct CLAUDE.md Hard Rule violation. Given this hard rule is stated explicitly and repeatedly in
project instructions, a compliance rate under 90% across a full week is worth a closer look at which
specific dispatches skip it — possibly a prompt-template gap in a specific workflow phase rather than
inconsistent agent behavior. The `dod_condition_failed` reason code continuing to dominate gate
failures (as also noted in the prior W33 pass) suggests Plan/Review still under-anticipate certain DoD
conditions (particularly "no material gap left unstated" requiring a *ticket-level*, not just
staging-artifact-level, disclosure with a follow-up-ticket reference) — this session's own tickets hit
this exact gap twice (the harvest-craft-event doc-staleness deferral, and would have hit it a third
time on the SIMQ-COGNITION ticket's AC5 shortfall had the disclosure not already been written into the
ticket body proactively).

**What worked.** Gate Integrity held with zero exceptions across this session's 4 tickets — every
blocking verdict was resolved with real substance or transparently disclosed via a filed follow-up
ticket, never edited-to-pass. `Test` (26/26 ok), `Document-Update` (23/23 ok), and `Finalize` (27/27
ok) all ran clean at 100% across the full week, confirming these phases remain reliable once a plan
clears Review. The `CommittedIntention` ticket's golden-SHA256-hash architecture guard — proving a
sensitive arbiter function stayed byte-identical — was independently recomputed by 3 separate review
passes (pre-Implement Plan review, post-Implement Architecture-Verify, and Verify) and matched every
time; this is a strong, reusable pattern worth carrying into future durable-state tickets that need to
prove "no behavior change to X while extending Y."
