## Notes

**Scope of this retro:** W33 covers both the tail of the `cognition-adventure-eligibility` batch (Aug 10) and the full 10-ticket `adventure-cognition-merge` epic (Aug 11-12, run_ids `TCK-20260811-*` + the `FOLDER-tickets-todos-adventure-cognition-merge` batch record). The index was stale (built before this session's runs landed) and undercounted 32 real W33 runs as 18 — rebuilt via `make agent-monitoring-index` before generating this report; always do this before trusting a same-session retro.

**1. What failed most?** `architecture-reviewer` (the agent behind both Review and Architecture-Verify): 22/52 calls week-wide returned `NEEDS_CHANGES` (42%); within the epic alone, 16/36 (44%) — every epic ticket except `RELATIONSHIP-AWARE-FORM-PARTY` needed at least one correction round. A specific defect shape recurred **4 separate times** across 3 different tickets: doc/parity-ledger prose citing a `STRAT-*`/`SOC-*` entry ("see STRAT-255...") that didn't exist yet at the time Document-Update wrote it — `SOCIAL-CONTRACT-GOAL-SCORER` (STRAT-254/SOC-208 cited before landing), `REGION-STABILIZATION-GOAL-SCORER` (twice: STRAT-255 cited before landing, then a wrong line-number in the same entry after it did land), and `CAPABILITY-CONFIDENCE-ADVENTURE-SCORING` (a "Not yet live" cross-reference pointing at a section that didn't say that). Root cause: plan.md pre-drafts the exact doc prose including the citation, Document-Update transcribes it, but the actual parity-ledger entry isn't created until the later Parity phase — so any citation written during Document-Update is structurally unverifiable at the time it's written, and only caught reactively by the next Architecture-Verify pass, costing a full correction round each time.

**2. What was slow?** `Investigate` has the highest per-event spend-proxy average (193.2) of any phase, but the real epic-specific overrun was correction-round churn, not Investigate itself: the 3 largest duration outliers this week (`REGION-STABILIZATION-GOAL-SCORER` 4.8x tier-median, `DELETE-ADVENTURE-DECISION-PHASE` 4.7x, `ADVENTURE-GOAL-SCORER` 3.3x) were exactly the tickets with the most Review/Architecture-Verify/Verify round-trips (3, 3, and 1 correction rounds respectively, plus session-limit interruptions on 2 of them that added idle wall-clock time not reflective of actual work).

**3. What to change?** When a plan requires doc prose to cite a not-yet-created parity-ledger ID, Document-Update should either (a) defer to a generic phrase ("documented in the parity ledger, see Parity phase") instead of a specific ID it can't yet verify, or (b) the orchestrator should create the real parity-ledger entry in the same pass right before Document-Update runs, so any citation Document-Update writes is checkable against a real entry immediately. Either would have prevented 4 of this week's 16 within-epic `NEEDS_CHANGES` verdicts — a meaningful fraction of the round-trip cost.

**4. What worked?** `Test` was 100% clean week-wide (22/22 `ok`) — by the time work reached Test, Review and Architecture-Verify had already caught the substantive issues, which is exactly where that cost should land. Gate Integrity held throughout: every one of the epic's 16 `NEEDS_CHANGES` verdicts and 2 `done-checker` `BLOCKED` verdicts (both "Files Changed / Completion Summary omitted a real, disclosed finding" — `DELETE-ADVENTURE-DECISION-PHASE` and `RELATIONSHIP-AWARE-FORM-PARTY`) were resolved with a genuine fix and independently re-verified by a fresh reviewer pass, never routed around. All 10 epic tickets reached `DONE`; zero shipped with an unresolved gate finding. Parity-ledger false-positive discrimination (file-path hints ruled out for `social_narrative.yaml`/`progression.yaml`/`infrastructure.yaml` on multiple tickets) was consistently correct — no over-fixing, no missed true positives.
