**2. What was slow?** `Investigate` carries the highest average cost-proxy (195.7) and `Implement` the second-highest (165.3) — both expected, since these are the two phases that do the actual reading/writing work. One extreme outlier: `TCK-20260811-SIMQ-COGNITION-BAND-CROSSING-DA-AND-LIFECYCLE-ANCHOR-GAP`'s Implement-phase call scored 1450.6 against a phase median of 93.4 (15.5x) — this ticket also tops the duration-outlier table at 7.1x its tier median (630 min total). This is the single clearest candidate for review if implementer-prompt cost is ever budgeted more tightly; nothing in this week's data suggests the outlier was wasted work (the ticket closed DONE), but it's worth a follow-up look at whether that Implement phase could have been split into two tickets.

**3. What to change?** Search-Before-Grep compliance during Investigate phase is 81.8% (18/22) — below 100%, meaning roughly 1 in 5 Investigate-phase agent calls this week did not call `search_docs`/`graphify query` before falling back to grep, despite this being a CLAUDE.md hard rule enforced by a `PreToolUse` hook reminder. Concrete action: the hook reminder fires correctly (visible on every `Agent`/`Bash` call in this session), but a reminder alone is not closing the gap — worth checking whether the 4 non-compliant calls were legitimate fallback-after-MCP-unavailable cases (permitted) or genuine skips, and if the latter, tightening the investigator agent prompt's ordering language rather than relying on the hook alone.

**4. What worked?** `doc-updater` (23/23 ok), `test-scoper` (23/23 ok), `investigator` (19/19 ok), and the `Investigate`/`Implement`/`Test`/`Document-Update`/`Finalize` phases (0 failures each at the phase-status level) all had clean weeks — no agent-status or phase-status failures. The multi-pass rescoping pattern used this week on `TCK-20260813-ADVENTURE-ROUTE-LAST-ROUTING-FAMILY-RESTORE` (first Verify pass correctly returned `DOD_BLOCKED` on a genuinely unmet, causally-entangled AC; an honest rescoping with a real follow-up ticket filed; second Verify pass independently confirmed `DOD_PASS`) is a confirmed-good pattern worth keeping as-is — it shows up in this week's `Verify` phase split (29 ok / 11 failed / 2 blocked) without needing a process change, since the failures there are doing their intended job of blocking premature closes rather than indicating a broken gate.
