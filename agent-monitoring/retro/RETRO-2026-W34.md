## Notes

**1. What failed most?** `docs_to_update_coverage` (a `done_checker_static` sub-check under the
`Verify`/DOD_BLOCKED path) is the single most common gate-failure root cause this week, though it
doesn't show as its own row above — it's folded into `DOD_BLOCKED`/`dod_condition_failed`. Direct
evidence from this session's own world-rendering-core batch: at least 2 of 9 tickets
(`VISUAL-VARIANTS-METRIC`, and pre-emptively caught before it could recur on
`VISUAL-GRADE-SCORER`) hit the exact same false-positive — `investigation.md`'s "Docs Requiring
Update" section lists an **excluded** doc as a leading `` - `docs/...` `` bullet (matching the
parser's "required path" pattern) instead of prose, because the doc's own reasoning text ("is
**not** listed...") reads as excluded to a human but the parser only reads the leading backtick
path. **Action: fix the `investigator` agent's own template/instructions to default to prose for
excluded docs, not bullets** — this would prevent the pattern at the source instead of relying on
each ticket's orchestrator to catch and reformat it after the fact, which worked but cost a wasted
BLOCKED→fix→re-verify round trip on `VARIANTS-METRIC`.

**2. What was slow?** Four standard/hotfix runs are dramatic duration outliers relative to their
own tier's median: `TCK-20260817-DOC-CONCURRENCY-LIMIT-RATIONALE` (470 min, **31.4x** the
hotfix median), `TCK-20260819-STANDARD-PARITY-LEDGER-HYGIENE-SWEEP` (434 min, 9.9x),
`TCK-20260817-RUNTIMEMODE-BENCH-SCOPING` (314 min, 7.2x), `TCK-20260817-STATE-DESIGN-PRIORITY-ORDER`
(264 min, 6.0x) — all four finished `DONE`, so this isn't a quality problem, but a 31x outlier on
a *hotfix*-tier ticket is worth a closer look next retro: was it genuinely complex work
mis-classified as hotfix-tier, or a stall/retry loop the duration metric can't distinguish from
real work?

**3. What to change?** Search-before-grep compliance is 75% (18/24 Investigate-phase calls) —
one in four Investigate phases this week skipped or under-used `search_docs`/`graphify` before
falling back to grep, and the non-compliant group's read-count is 2.6x higher (38.3 avg vs. 14.5
avg) than the compliant group — direct evidence that skipping the semantic-search step costs more
raw reading later, not less. Some of this session's own Investigate phases (world-rendering-core
batch, tickets 7-9) hit `search_docs`'s `index not found` error and correctly fell back per
CLAUDE.md's documented exception — that's a legitimate, logged case, not non-compliance — but the
75% figure suggests other sessions may be skipping the step without hitting a real tool outage.
Worth spot-checking whether the index-unavailable condition has been persistent enough this week
