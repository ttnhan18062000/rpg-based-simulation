This folder is a placeholder, filed now so the roadmap's full 6-phase structure is represented under
`tickets/todos/`, matching the other five phase folders. It should stay empty of tickets until Phases
2–4 (`simq-roadmap-phase2-depth-social/`, `simq-roadmap-phase3-depth-faction-information/`,
`simq-roadmap-phase4-depth-cognition/`) have landed or Phase 4 has reported its investigation-only
finding.

**What happens here, when the time comes:** a short decision doc (not a full re-plan) answering,
with the real observed cost-per-world-per-pillar from the three depth waves: is continuing toward
full 17-world coverage worth it, or does the corpus's current staged depth constitute the final
"near-perfect" bar for this feature? Either answer should be recorded the same way the AGENCY-DA
ruling was — a documented decision. This may not even need a formal ticket (a doc-only hotfix, or an
entry in `docs/guidelines/intentional_divergences.md`, may be sufficient) — that determination itself
is part of what happens at this gate, not decided now.

See `docs/plans/simq_development_roadmap.md` §"Phase 5" for the roadmap author's own non-binding
recommendation (staged depth likely being the right permanent bar) — explicitly flagged there as a
guess to revisit with real data, not a decision made in advance.
