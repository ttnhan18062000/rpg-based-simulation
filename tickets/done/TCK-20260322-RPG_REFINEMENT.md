# TCK-20260322-RPG_REFINEMENT

## Title
Comprehensive RPG Logic & Thinking Refinement (Milestones 4-6)

## Description
This ticket covers the transition from a "Robotic/Reactive" simulation to a "Living/Proactive" RPG world. It implements the 4 Pillars of Refinement (Soul, Body, Action, Social) and the 7-step Cognitive Pipeline.

## Scope
- **The Soul**: Cognitive Pipeline, Personality traits, Emotional states, Selective attention.
- **The Body**: Genetic diversity, Attribute aptitudes, Breakthroughs, Talent branching.
- **The Action**: Tactical combat, Stances, Hysteresis-driven movement, Loop prevention.
- **The Social**: Inter-hero economy, Intel sharing, Reputation-based pricing.
- **The Timeline**: 100k-tick lifecycle (Incarnation to Legend), Narrative memory.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AIBrain refactored into a 4-phase decoupled pipeline.
- [ ] entities demonstrate "Generous Thinking" (no oscillation at boundaries).
- [ ] Attributes grow with individual aptitudes and breakthrough caps.
- [ ] Heroes remember past events (Trauma/Glory) affecting future weights.
- [ ] Full TDD suite covering behavioral transitions.
