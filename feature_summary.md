| Feature                                       | Keep / Add / Strengthen | Why                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

## My blunt recommendation

The **top 5 major features** should be:

1. **Phase Guard / Domain Permission System**
2. **Scenario Runtime Service**
3. **Domain Ownership + Capability Registry**
4. **Long-Horizon Behavior Regression**
5. **Decision Explanation / Intent Trace Service**

Do **not** start with narrative, nemesis, heirlooms, or more RPG content yet. Those are attractive, but they sit on top of architecture. The current risk is not “lack of cool RPG logic”; the risk is **too many systems without one enforceable runtime contract**.
